How should each island be built?

Soft Lagoon palette applied. All three fix the "flat platform + scattered props" problem and the water-breaking bug the same way — a soft rounded shoreline ring that sits in the water, not a hard-edged platform floating above it. They differ in what the "hero feature" at the center is. Every biome (jungle, desert, tundra, volcano, reef, highlands) would reuse whichever pattern you pick, just re-themed.

Rock & Tide Pool

A rounded rock outcrop with a trickling pool at its base, driftwood at the shore. Reads as "a little wild place," works well for jungle/highlands/volcano biomes.

Palm Knoll

A soft grassy mound with one leaning signature tree and round bushes either side. Reads as classic "desert island," works well for desert/reef/tundra biomes.

Reef Garden

A flatter coral-and-anemone garden capping a low sandy mound — no tall centerpiece. Reads as calm and horizontal, works well for reef/tundra, less so for volcano/highlands.

You can also pick more than one — e.g. "Rock & Tide Pool" for jungle/volcano/highlands and "Palm Knoll" for desert/reef/tundra.
